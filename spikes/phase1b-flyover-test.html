Phase 1b — flyover rendering test, at real display sizes

Testing whether the "draped fabric" terrain look (flat vector basemap displaced over a coarse DEM mesh — see docs/roadmap-3d-flyover.md) reads differently at the sizes this would actually render at on the site, vs. a full desktop window. The OSM viewpoint POI icons ("Rattlesnake Ledge" etc.) that didn't line up with the terrain are stripped from the basemap here — those were never our data, just OpenFreeMap's generic POI layer.

This is not the final camera (that's Phase 3 — an algorithmic path with tuned pitch/lookahead). This just follows the real rattlesnake-ledge.gpx track with a simple heading-facing camera, enough motion to judge terrain quality while animated rather than from a static shot.
